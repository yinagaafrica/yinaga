Our Partners

Real collaborations already driving YINAGA's programmes on the ground in Jigawa State and beyond.

The Alternative Bank

Financial partner for the Improved Seed Empowerment & Financial Inclusion Programme in Dabi Local Government Area, Jigawa State, reaching over 100 farmers.

Tecni Seeds

Seed and agri-input partner for the Improved Seed Empowerment Programme and the YINAGA Outgrower Programme, which began cultivation across 30 hectares of farmland.

Federal University Dutse

Host institution and academic partner for the YINAGA Agrinnovation Summit, which brought together over 200 students from agricultural and non-agricultural disciplines.

NAJISS-FUD

National Association of Jigawa State Students, FUD Chapter. Student mobilisation partner supporting outreach and turnout for YINAGA programmes across the university.

NAAS

National Association of Agricultural Students. Connects YINAGA directly with agriculture-focused student communities and emerging agricultural talent.
