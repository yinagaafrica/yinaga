Flagship Initiatives

Each project is designed to create lasting, scalable change for African youth in agriculture.

🌾

Initiative 01

Youth Agribusiness Launchpad

A structured programme providing young Nigerians with agribusiness training, access to micro-funding, and mentorship from established agripreneurs. Target: 10,000 youth-led businesses operational by end of 2026.

38% of 2026 target reached

📱

Initiative 02

Smart Farming & AgriTech Drive

Equipping youth with digital tools — drone mapping, soil sensors, mobile market platforms — to modernise smallholder farming and boost productivity across rural communities.

22% of 2026 target reached

♀️

Initiative 03

Women in Agriculture Programme

A dedicated strand ensuring young women receive equal access to training, land, funding, and leadership positions within YINAGA and the wider agribusiness sector.

51% of 2026 target reached

♻️

Initiative 04

Circular Agriculture & Waste Recovery

Training youth to convert agricultural waste into compost, biogas, and value-added products — building circular economy models that are profitable and environmentally responsible.

15% of 2026 target reached

🤝

Initiative 05

State Coordinator Network

Building a grassroots coordinator network across all 36 Nigerian states — each coordinator driving local recruitment, training events, and liaison with state ministries of agriculture.

8% of states with active coordinators

🌍

Initiative 06

Pan-African Youth Agriculture Summit

An annual continental summit bringing together youth agripreneurs, policymakers, investors, and research institutions to share knowledge, forge partnerships, and celebrate African agricultural excellence.

2025 Summit completed — 2026 edition in planning
