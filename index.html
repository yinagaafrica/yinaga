President’s Message

"Africa's future will not be built by waiting for opportunities, it will be built by young people bold enough to create them through agriculture. Agriculture is one of the most powerful tools we have to fight hunger, create jobs and build stability in Africa. I believe in one simple vision: a peaceful and prosperous Africa powered by youth, driven by agriculture. This is not just a vision, it is possible."

Ayodeji Adegboro
President, YINAGA

Director General’s Message

"At YINAGA, we believe the solution to Africa’s food security lies in the hands of bold, innovative young people willing to turn challenges into opportunities. Our mission is clear, to inspire, equip, and position young Africans to become the driving force behind a resilient, innovative, and prosperous agricultural future."

Stanley Chukwuka
Director General, YINAGA
